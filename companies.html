Publishing

003

Independent editorial brands with a direct relationship to their readers, funded to take longer than a quarter to prove a list or a beat.

No.CompanySectorStatusThesis
001 Northbound Press Publishing Founded 2018 An independent nonfiction imprint that pays advances a corporate house wouldn't.

Northbound Press publishes narrative nonfiction and reported memoir, releasing twelve to fifteen titles a year. It pays advances more typical of a Big Five imprint while keeping a staff of nine, funded entirely by permanent capital rather than a distribution deal that dictates its list.

002 The Marginal Line Publishing Acquired 2021 A subscriber-funded policy magazine read by staff who write the regulations it covers.

The Marginal Line is a subscriber-funded magazine covering regulation, trade, and industrial policy for a readership of policy staff and institutional investors. KVIAZ acquired it from its founding editor in 2021 to fund a larger reporting staff without introducing a majority owner who'd want a return by a set date.

003 Almanac Quarterly Publishing Acquired 2024 A print review journal for fiction in translation, kept quarterly and kept in print.

Almanac Quarterly is a print-first review journal covering fiction in translation. KVIAZ acquired the title in 2024 after its previous publisher folded, and committed to keep it quarterly and in print — the two things a corporate buyer would likely have cut first.

Artificial Intelligence

002

Applied AI products built for one narrow, durable job each, sold to customers too specific for a general-purpose model provider to chase.

No.CompanySectorStatusThesis
004 Aperture Labs Artificial Intelligence Founded 2020 Document extraction for insurance claims, done to 99.6% field-level accuracy.

Aperture Labs builds document-extraction models for property and casualty insurers, turning scanned claims correspondence into structured data at 99.6% field-level accuracy. It serves eleven regional insurers and has never taken on a general-purpose product line.

005 Fathom Artificial Intelligence Acquired 2022 Transcription and captioning infrastructure for regional broadcasters.

Fathom provides transcription and captioning infrastructure to regional broadcasters and public radio stations, a market too small for the large speech-AI vendors to prioritize. KVIAZ acquired Fathom from its two co-founders in 2022 to fund compliance certifications the company had been quoting for three years.

Commerce

002

Category-specific commerce businesses with real inventory, real logistics, and margin discipline instead of a growth subsidy.

No.CompanySectorStatusThesis
006 Harlow & Finch Commerce Founded 2017 Specialty espresso equipment, sold with a repair program most retailers won't offer.

Harlow & Finch sells and services specialty espresso equipment directly to independent cafés, with a repair program that keeps machines running well past the point most retailers would rather sell a replacement. It holds its own inventory and technicians in four regional markets.

007 Fieldstock Commerce Acquired 2019 A resale marketplace for technical outdoor gear, reconditioned before relisting.

Fieldstock operates a resale marketplace for technical outdoor gear, authenticating and reconditioning each item before it's relisted. KVIAZ acquired the company in 2019 from a founder who needed working capital for inventory, not an acquirer for the business itself.

Media

002

Audio and film properties built around a single format or voice, released on a fixed schedule rather than a growth-driven one.

No.CompanySectorStatusThesis
008 Wavelength Audio Media Founded 2019 Three long-running narrative podcasts, not a feed of formulaic ones.

Wavelength Audio produces three long-running narrative podcasts, each built around a single host and released on a fixed weekly schedule rather than a growth-driven publishing calendar. It has turned down four network-style expansion pitches since 2019.

009 Sixth Floor Media Acquired 2023 A documentary unit funded for multi-year investigative films.

Sixth Floor is a documentary production unit specializing in feature-length investigative films, acquired in 2023 when its founding producers wanted funding for a slower, multi-year production model that streaming commissioners wouldn't underwrite.
