Why authenticity matters

A great product is only a good purchase if you receive the product you intended to buy.

The product itself is only part of the purchase. Where it comes from, how it was stored, and what happens if something goes wrong all shape whether the purchase turns out the way you expected.

Worth considering

  • Seller identity
  • Product authenticity
  • Product condition
  • Shipping origin
  • Storage and handling
  • Return policies
  • Customer support

01 / Amazon

Amazon: selection and convenience, with one important variable.

Amazon offers enormous selection and convenience, but the seller matters. A single search can surface listings sold directly by Amazon, by third-party sellers, and under different fulfillment arrangements — and those listings don't all represent the same purchasing experience.

"On Amazon, don't just check the product. Check who is selling it."

Shop at Amazon →

Before buying on Amazon

  • Check the seller.
  • Check whether the product is fulfilled by Amazon, or otherwise understand the fulfillment arrangement.
  • Look for the official brand storefront when available.
  • Review the product listing carefully.
  • Check the return policy.
  • Look for appropriate authenticity signals.

02 / YesStyle

YesStyle: broad K-Beauty selection through a centralized retail experience.

YesStyle is a major online retailer with a broad selection of Asian beauty products, including a wide range of Korean brands. For K-Beauty shoppers, it offers a centralized retail experience — one storefront and one checkout, rather than piecing a purchase together across separate sellers.

Shop at YesStyle →

Still worth checking

  • Product availability
  • Shipping time
  • Shipping cost
  • Regional availability
  • Return conditions
  • Total purchase cost

03 / Olive Young

Olive Young: direct access to a major Korean beauty retailer.

Olive Young is a major Korean health and beauty retailer, and Olive Young Global extends that selection to shoppers outside Korea. For K-Beauty specifically, it offers direct access to the Korean retail market and the brands that shape it.

Shop at Olive Young →

Still worth checking

  • Shipping from Korea
  • Shipping cost
  • Delivery time
  • Country availability
  • Promotions
  • Returns
