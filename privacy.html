Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

We believe trust is earned through transparency. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it — in plain language, not legal filler.

Last updated August 8, 2026
01

Introduction

KVIAZ is a holding company — we build, acquire, and operate digital businesses across publishing, artificial intelligence, commerce, and media. This policy covers kviaz.com and the properties we operate directly under the KVIAZ name.

Current and future companies within the KVIAZ ecosystem keep their own product decisions, including their own privacy practices. Where a registry company handles your data separately from us, its own policy will say so and will govern that relationship.

What follows is the same standard we hold ourselves to internally: collect only what a purpose requires, explain that purpose plainly, and give you a real say in it.

02

Information we collect

Information you provide

When you contact us, subscribe to the Journal, or reach out about the registry, you may share your name, email address, company, and the contents of your message. We use this only to respond to you.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we automatically log technical details when you visit — your IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page, and timestamps. This helps us understand how the site is used and keep it running reliably.

Third-party services

Some information is collected on our behalf by the services listed in Section 6 — for analytics, security, and content delivery. Each operates under its own privacy policy, which we link to below.

03

How we use information

We use the information above for a short, specific list of purposes:

  • Operate and improve kviaz.com
  • Respond to inquiries from founders, press, and visitors
  • Understand how the site is used, in aggregate
  • Keep the Journal and registry pages relevant and accurate
  • Maintain the security and integrity of our systems

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

04

Affiliate disclosure

Some pages across KVIAZ and our registry companies may contain affiliate links. If you follow one of these links and make a purchase, KVIAZ or the relevant company may earn a commission.

There is never any additional cost to you for using an affiliate link — the price is the same either way.

Recommendations are made on their own merits. A commission never determines what we recommend or how we describe it.

05

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies, grouped by what they do:

  • Necessary — required for the site to function, such as remembering security settings.
  • Analytics — help us understand aggregate traffic and usage patterns.
  • Performance — help pages load reliably and quickly.
  • Marketing — used sparingly, and only where noted, to measure the reach of something we've published.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

06

Third-party services

We rely on a small set of established providers to run the site and reach our audience. Each is bound by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Associates Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Instagram Privacy Policy
07

Data security

We apply reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect the information we hold — including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular review of our systems.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If that ever changes for information we hold, we'll tell you.

08

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct information that's inaccurate or incomplete
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of analytics or marketing where applicable
  • Exercise any additional rights granted by your local privacy law

To exercise any of these, write to us at the address in Section 10 — we'll respond promptly.

09

Children's privacy

KVIAZ is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

10

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request regarding your information, can go directly to us.
