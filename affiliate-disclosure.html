Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency first.

Some recommendations on KVIAZ may generate a commission when you make a purchase. Our commitment to honest, independent recommendations never changes.

Last updated August 8, 2026
01

Why this page exists

Transparency is something we ask of every company in our registry, so we hold kviaz.com to the same standard. This page explains, plainly, how affiliate partnerships fit into how we operate.

Affiliate relationships help support independent publishing — including the reporting and reviews we invest in across the registry. You deserve to know that, and to know it changes nothing about what we recommend.

02

How affiliate links work

Some links on kviaz.com and across our registry are affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, the retailer may pay KVIAZ or the relevant company a small commission.

You pay exactly the same price either way. An affiliate link never marks up a product or adds a fee — the commission comes from the retailer's own marketing budget, not your pocket.

03

Editorial independence

This is the part that matters most, so we'll say it directly:

  • Affiliate commissions never determine rankings
  • Affiliate commissions never influence a recommendation
  • A negative review is published whenever one is deserved
  • Products are evaluated before revenue is ever considered
  • Reader trust comes before affiliate income, every time

If a product doesn't hold up, we say so — whether or not it pays us to.

04

How products are selected

Something earns a place in our coverage because of:

  • Quality
  • Performance
  • Innovation
  • Customer experience
  • Reliability
  • Value
  • Long-term usefulness

Never because it pays a higher commission. Two products with identical merit and different payouts get identical treatment.

05

Affiliate partners

KVIAZ currently works, or may work, with a small set of trusted affiliate programs, including Amazon Associates, YesStyle, and Olive Young.

This list evolves as the registry grows — new platforms are added deliberately, and this page is kept current when they are.

06

FTC compliance

KVIAZ follows the Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on affiliate and endorsement disclosures. Where a specific piece of content includes affiliate links, we disclose it clearly, close to where it appears — not just here.

If you ever come across a link you feel wasn't disclosed clearly enough, tell us. We'd rather fix it than argue about it.

07

No sponsored opinions

A company cannot purchase a positive review from us, at any price. Advertising a company may place with us never changes an editorial conclusion.

Where content is sponsored, it's labeled as sponsored, in plain terms, so it's never mistaken for independent coverage.

08

Our promise

If we recommend it, it's because we'd recommend it to our own family.

Trust is worth more than any single commission — we would rather lose the revenue than lose your confidence in what we publish.

The KVIAZ Editorial Promise

Every recommendation published by KVIAZ follows the same editorial principles, regardless of affiliate relationships or commercial partnerships.

  • We earn commissions. We never sell recommendations.
  • We compare before we recommend.
  • We disclose every affiliate relationship.
  • We never sell rankings or editorial opinions.
  • We correct mistakes when we find them.
  • We would rather lose revenue than lose your trust.
09

Changes to this Disclosure

Affiliate programs and partnerships evolve, and we'll update this page as they do. When we make a material change, we'll reflect it by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10

Contact

Questions about an affiliate relationship, or a specific piece of content, can go directly to us.
