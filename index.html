How we operate

Three commitments that don't change with the market.

01

Permanent capital

We don't raise funds with a maturity date. Capital committed to a company stays with it — through slow years as well as good ones.

02

Operating autonomy

Each company keeps its own name, its own team, and its own product decisions. We do not centralize brands or force cross-selling.

03

Long-cycle judgment

We measure decisions in years, not quarters, and we're comfortable being unremarkable in the interim while a business compounds.
