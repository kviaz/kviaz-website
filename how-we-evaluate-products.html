Why it exists

A score you can actually see the reasoning behind.

The beauty industry is full of ratings and recommendations, most of them unexplained. A star average doesn't tell you what was tested, what wasn't, or why one product outranked another.

The KVIAZ Trust Score™ exists to make that reasoning visible — the same framework behind every product we recommend, so you can see not only what we recommend, but why.

How we weigh evidence

Not all evidence is equal.

KVIAZ considers many kinds of evidence — scientific research, product-specific information, manufacturer documentation, our own structured testing, and real consumer experience. We don't treat them the same way.

  • Ingredient evidence doesn't automatically prove a finished product will work the same way.
  • Manufacturer claims are identified as manufacturer claims, not independent evidence.
  • Community reviews can reveal useful patterns — they aren't treated as scientific proof.
  • When evidence is limited, we say so, rather than filling the gap with confidence we don't have.

Built by category

The framework stays the same. The criteria adapt.

A sunscreen isn't evaluated the same way as a cleanser or a serum. Formula, Evidence, Value and Purchase Confidence apply consistently across every category — but what "good performance" means shifts with what the product is actually for.

Editorial independence

A product receives the same evaluation whether or not KVIAZ earns a commission from its purchase. Affiliate links are how we fund this work — they are a monetization mechanism, not an evaluation criterion.
